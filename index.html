FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Shapeshift free?+

Yes. Shapeshift runs entirely in your browser with no account, download or paywall.

What is half-slab detail?+

Curves are sampled twice per block vertically. When a curve cuts through a block, Shapeshift places a bottom or top slab instead of a full block, halving the stair-stepping. Exports keep the exact slab block state.

Can I export to Litematica or WorldEdit?+

Yes. Shapeshift exports Litematica .litematic files, Sponge v2 .schem files for WorldEdit and FAWE, vanilla structure block .nbt files, block lists and layer PNGs.

Are the Minecraft textures included?+

No texture files are bundled. The texture toggle loads Minecraft 1.20.1 block textures at runtime from a public asset mirror. If the mirror is unavailable, Shapeshift falls back to flat colours.

Which Minecraft version does Shapeshift target?+

Block states target Minecraft 1.20.1, data version 3465. Litematica, Sponge and structure-block exports include the appropriate slab properties.

Does it work on mobile?+

Yes. The viewer supports touch orbit and pinch zoom, and the controls collapse into drawers. It is still happiest on a tablet or desktop.
